## What each case protects

**`base_shipment`** — The as-received SHP-4471 document set. Regression baseline for the whole catalogue.

**`fully_conforming`** — Every term met. Guards against a catalogue that only knows how to say no. If this case ever goes red, the rules have become over-tight and ops will stop trusting the queue.

**`moisture_above_allowance_ceiling`** — Moisture 15.40% breaches the 15.00% ceiling. This is a rejection right, not an allowance. Getting this boundary wrong either forfeits a rejection right or wrongly rejects a payable cargo.

**`quantity_below_tolerance`** — 55,000 MT shipped against a 57,000-63,000 MT contractual range. Short-loading below tolerance is a contractual breach, not a pro-rata adjustment.

**`oil_below_contractual_minimum`** — Average oil 18.20% against an 18.50% minimum. Confidence raised to 0.95 so the assertion tests the rule, not the confidence gate.

**`oil_single_determination_low_average_conforms`** — FALSE-REJECTION GUARD. Determination 2 (18.40%) is below the 18.50% minimum on its own, but contract cl.6 makes oil content the average of three determinations, and the average (18.70%) conforms. A naive per-determination read rejects a conforming panamax cargo worth USD 27m. This is the single highest-cost way to get this catalogue wrong.

**`wrong_bushel_conversion_factor`** — Contract carries 39.3683 bu/MT, the maize factor (56 lb/bu), against a soyabean cargo (60 lb/bu, 36.7437). Produces a plausible-looking price that is wrong by roughly USD 30/MT. PRC-03 re-derives rather than trusting the stated flat price, so it catches this.

**`low_confidence_bl_weight`** — Same conforming weight, but extracted at 0.60 confidence (smudged bulk B/L). Both quantity rules must withhold their verdict rather than assert a pass on a number the pipeline cannot read. Tests the confidence gate itself.

**`amendment_fully_authorised_still_reviewed`** — ACCEPTED LIMITATION, NOT A BUG. The extension is acknowledged by the contract signatory and confirmed by the broker, so cl.10 appears satisfied on the face of the chat. SHP-02 still routes to a human. Whether a named individual holds delegated authority to bind Harbourline is master data the pipeline does not hold, and the downside of being wrong is an unenforceable USD 27m contract. Cost of this choice is quantified in evals/RESULTS.md.

**`claim_time_bar_not_tracked`** — KNOWN GAP, ASSERTED DELIBERATELY. The catalogue finds USD 184,294.60 of recoverable value but attaches no deadline to it. GAFTA 100 time-bars quality and condition claims; a credit note request that sits in a queue past the bar is worth zero. The pipeline models the money and not the clock.
